## Generation prompt

Create a wide landscape 3:2 grayscale source plate for a website ASCII-art animation. A close crop of exactly TWO hands and forearms in the iconic reaching-fingertips composition of Michelangelo's Creation of Adam. LEFT: a natural human right hand, anatomically beautiful, relaxed wrist entering from the far left lower edge, palm facing downward, index finger extended toward the center-right, remaining three fingers elegantly curled underneath and a natural thumb. RIGHT: an intricately engineered mechanical humanoid left hand, articulated metal finger joints and broad armor plates, wrist/forearm enters from far right upper edge, index finger extending diagonally down-left toward the human fingertip. Fingertips almost touch at the exact center, separated by a narrow gap, human finger slightly lower. Match the expressive relaxed silhouette of Creation of Adam, not a handshake and not two fists pointing. Both hands should be large, occupying the middle 65 percent of the image's height, with wrists continuing fully to the left and right image edges. ONLY the hands and short forearms; no people, faces, sleeves, text, logos, particles or scenery. Pure solid black background (#000000), no grey haze or vignette. Hands rendered as high-contrast silver-white monochrome sculptural forms, with broad midtone modeling and deep black creases to remain legible when sampled into a coarse ASCII character grid. Crisp silhouettes, clear knuckles, strong separation between curled fingers. The human hand has skin creases and tendons; the robotic hand has unmistakable geometric joints and layered mechanical plating. Dramatic Renaissance side lighting. This is not the final ASCII output: deliver detailed grayscale hands on black, no characters. Keep all human pixels left of the center vertical line and all mechanical pixels right of it for independent animation layers.

## Rendering

The hand renderer uses only printable ASCII characters, with one sample every approximately 5.2 CSS pixels, capped at 380 columns. This keeps individual glyphs visible while retaining finger and joint detail. Density is mapped from source luminance. Glyphs shift by less than a pixel and change density slowly; the opening's hand separation, logo scale and text reveal follow scroll position. The initial viewport contains the hands and the global theme switch in the upper-right corner. The galaxy starts appearing at 5% scroll progress; navigation appears at 72%, with the community introduction. The wordmark's complete letters settle into focus without a clipping mask. Both canvas renderers operate independently of interface controls and redraw their colors when the day/night theme changes. Off-screen/hidden tabs stop drawing. Reduced motion shows the final community cover directly, and a keyboard-focus skip link always reaches the introduction. There are no visible playback, scroll-prompt or opening-skip controls.
